# pen grip research
- Larger diameter keeps fingers more open instead of curled; a barrel around half an inch or more is a common suggestion.
- Check the grip's inner hole against the pen barrel. Foam sets sometimes ship in inner diameters near 25, 28 and 30 mm.
- Slip-on foam grips may not fit thicker pens; fit varies despite "one size fits most".
- Foam is soft and light; silicone is flexible, cleans easily and adds a larger hold.
- Many grips are marketed to children; adult-oriented options are less common.
- Strength-building claims on listings are marketing; do not repeat them.
- An occupational therapist can recommend sizes and designs where grip strength or arthritis is a concern.
