# soap dispenser research
- Glass looks upscale and shows soap level but is heavier and more fragile; plastic suits kids, cramped counters and wet hands. Hygiene claims for glass are vendor claims.
- Foaming pumps mix air into diluted soap; commonly cited soap to water ratios are about 1 to 3 through 1 to 5. Start around 1 to 4, adjust if foam is watery or the pump sticks.
- Foam pumps clog with thick, gel or exfoliating soaps and with castile in some reports. Do not put undiluted liquid soap in a foam pump.
- Manual pumps: cheaper, no power. Sensor pumps: touchless, need batteries or a charge, can misfire with thick soap or low battery.
- Check capacity (fewer refills), wide mouth for refilling, pump smoothness, stainless steel (304) vs plastic pump head, and non-slip base.
- Sources were retailer and buying guide pages; treat lifespans and hygiene claims as general.
