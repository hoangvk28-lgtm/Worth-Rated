# clothes hanger research
- Wood: sturdiest for heavy coats and blazers, keeps shape, helps avoid shoulder bumps; bulkier and costs more.
- Plastic: cheap and light; flat plastic flexes under load, tubular or thick plastic stronger.
- Metal: strong and slim; wire bends, rusts and can stain; rubber coated metal core is better; sharp edges can snag delicates.
- Velvet: best grip, slimmest profile, saves rod space (one review claims about 50% more garments on a rod); can collect lint, coating can wear; good for silk and satin.
- Listing weight capacities (around 10 lb for velvet and plastic) are per product claims, not standards.
- Choose by heaviest garment first, then fabric grip, then rod space.
- Pants bar with non slip surface; swivel hook helps access; notched shoulders for straps.
- Uniform hangers look tidy and hang clothes at one height.
