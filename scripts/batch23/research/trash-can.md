# trash can research
- Most kitchen cans hold about 13 gallons; standard kitchen bags fit 13-gallon cans. Larger sizes use commercial liners.
- Rough sizing: 10 to 13 gallons for one or two people, 13 or more for bigger households.
- Measure the floor footprint and confirm the lid can open fully in its spot (under cabinets, near walls).
- Odor control: tight-sealing lids, soft-close hinges, and optional carbon filters help; none removes odor if the bag is left too long.
- Step pedal cans are mechanical and simple; pedal durability varies. Sensor cans offer hands-free use but need batteries or power.
- Stainless steel resists dents and looks clean but shows fingerprints unless finished anti-fingerprint; plastic is lighter and cheaper.
- Bag ring or liner rim hides bag overhang and keeps the bag in place.
- Sources were retail and manufacturer guides (This Old House, iTouchless); treat as general guidance.
