# pen research
- Ballpoint uses oil-based paste ink: fast drying, low smear, resists bleed-through, suits receipts and carbonless paper; needs more pressure.
- Gel ink sits in the middle: smoother and bolder, needs less pressure, moderate drying time.
- Rollerball ink is the most liquid: smoothest, least pressure, slowest to set, most likely to smear and bleed through thin paper.
- Left-handers: fine tips (0.5mm) lay down less ink and dry faster than 0.7mm or 1.0mm; quick-dry gel formulas exist; test by dragging a hand across a line.
- Tip size: 0.5mm fine detail and small notes, 0.7mm everyday, 1.0mm bold and forgiving.
- Check the back of the page for bleed-through before committing to a pen for a journal.
- Retractable pens avoid cap loss; refillable pens (Pilot G2) lower long-term waste.
- Fountain pens: need cartridges or a converter; nib size (extra fine, fine, medium) changes line width; paper quality matters.
